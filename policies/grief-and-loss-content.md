# Grief and Loss Content Requests

# Summary

Covers tickets where customers ask for meditation or course content to help with death, grief, or loss. The primary recommendation is the **Befriending Grief with Vinnie Ferraro** course, which explicitly addresses death and loss. The opening tone is warm and grounded but avoids the standard "I'm so sorry for your loss" phrasing. These tickets are reply-only with no account changes. Do not merge sibling tickets for the same customer unless explicitly instructed.

# Trigger Conditions

- **Ticket signals:** customer asks for content about grief, death, loss, bereavement, mourning, or coping with the death of a loved one; customer mentions they are grieving or processing a loss; customer wants guided meditations or courses for grief
- **Account signals:** none required — these are content recommendation tickets, not account-dependent
- **Keywords / phrases:** "grief," "death," "loss," "died," "passed away," "mourning," "bereavement," "lost my," "coping with loss," "dealing with grief," "help with grief," "meditations for grief"

# Required Context

- [ ] Customer's request is specifically about death/loss grief (as opposed to other forms of loss like job loss, breakup, etc.)
- [ ] Subscription status — if unsubscribed, note that the course requires a subscription
- [ ] No indication that the customer is in acute crisis (if so, escalate per *Escalation Policy*)

# Policy / Correct Response

## Standard Case

Recommend the **Befriending Grief with Vinnie Ferraro** course. It is located under **Courses** in the app and explicitly talks about death and grief. Encourage the customer to explore it — it's designed for this kind of grief and loss.

**Opening tone:** Do NOT use "I'm so sorry for your loss." Instead, use grounded, warm language that acknowledges their struggle without assuming details:

> "I'm sorry to hear that that's something that you are struggling with. I'm glad you reached out."

**Sign-off:** Always use:

> Take care,  
> Happier Meditation Support Team

Never sign with a first name. Never sign as an individual agent.

## Variations

- **Unsubscribed customer:** Still recommend the course by name, and note that it requires a subscription. If they express interest, offer the standard trial or subscription information (see *Free Trial Policy* or *Subscription & Billing Overview*).
- **Customer already tried the course and wants more:** There is no second grief-specific course currently. Acknowledge that Befriending Grief is the primary resource, and log their request for additional grief content as feedback (see *Feedback Policy*).
- **Customer mentions a specific type of loss that isn't death** (e.g., pet loss, divorce, job loss): The Befriending Grief course focuses on death and bereavement, but grief principles are applicable. You may still recommend it with that context, or suggest they explore the general library. Use judgment.

## Edge Cases & Exceptions

- **Customer appears to be in acute crisis or severe distress:** Do not treat as a standard content request. Escalate per *Escalation Policy*.
- **Customer mentions multiple open tickets for the same email:** Do NOT merge sibling tickets unless explicitly instructed by a senior agent. Leave them separate.
- **Customer asks about grief content in a ticket that also involves a billing or account issue:** Handle both — address the billing/account issue first per the relevant policy, then recommend the course as a closing note.

# Action Classification

## No Action Required (reply only)

All standard grief content requests are reply-only. No account changes, billing adjustments, or subscription grants are automatically triggered. If the customer is unsubscribed and cannot afford a subscription, they may separately qualify for a complimentary subscription (see *Need-Based Complimentary Subscriptions*), but that requires human review and is not bundled into the content recommendation reply.

## Human Action Required

None for standard content recommendations.

## Do Not Auto-Send Conditions

- Customer language suggests acute crisis, suicidal ideation, or severe distress — escalate, do not send a standard reply
- Emotionally intense or vulnerable tone that requires careful calibration — flag for human review
- **High-touch emotional grief tickets** (deeply vulnerable, person-centered grief, domestic abuse contexts) — hold for Cassidy review even when the CONTENT (recommending Befriending Grief) follows policy. See top-level Solo vs Ping guidance (CLAUDE.md, decision 2026-09-02). Standard grief content requests that are not emotionally intense are solo-sendable.

## Escalation Triggers

- Customer is in crisis or expresses severe emotional distress beyond standard grief — see *Escalation Policy*
